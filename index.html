Our Philosophy

The Alchemy of Glass & Light

"A mirror is not merely a surface of reflection, but a gateway to depth, capturing the soul of a room's atmosphere."

At Misty, we fuse ancient silvering techniques with high-end modern minimalism. Every frame is hand-welded from premium brass, stainless steel, or carved from selected sustainably sourced white oak.

Our mirrors feature low-iron opti-white glass, ensuring zero color distortion and maximum luminosity. With integrated high-CRI LED backlighting, we introduce an ambient celestial glow that defines luxury living.

Opti-White Glass

99% iron-free raw glass for perfectly crisp, ultra-clear reflections.

Hand-Welded Finishes

Seamless edges sculpted by master metalworkers and joiners.

Handcrafted Luxury

Designed in Copenhagen. Engineered to capture light beautifully in any spatial context.
